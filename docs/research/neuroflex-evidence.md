# NeuroFlex evidence ledger

This ledger records research-informed instructional techniques considered for Shravya. It does not claim that the integrated Shravya intervention has been independently validated or clinically proven.

| Learner barrier | Instructional technique | Evidence source | Shravya interface translation | Safety boundary | Measurable learner behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Choosing how to approach a lesson | Learner choice, autonomy, adjustable support, explicit goals | CAST UDL Guidelines 3.0 | Support-choice screen and clear journey preview | No diagnosis inference or fixed disability mode | Chooses, changes, or resumes a support preference |
| Organising a multistep task | Visual supports and task analysis | NCAEP 2020 Evidence-Based Practices | Ordered concept flow and recovery orientation | Use only teacher-approved lesson concepts | Identifies previous, current, and next lesson concept |
| Needing a next-step prompt | Prompting and self-management | NCAEP 2020 Evidence-Based Practices | Teacher-verified cue and optional retry | No punitive score, timer, or inferred profile | Opens a cue and returns to the current concept action |
| Planning and checking middle-school work | Planning, organisation, prompting, modelling, checklists, self-monitoring | IES HOPS middle-school research (Grades 6–8) | Small-step preview, pause/resume, and learner-controlled support | Stored state describes journey progress, not intelligence | Resumes a saved step or changes support |
| Connecting an abstract idea to an accessible representation | Worked examples, visual-verbal representations, concrete/abstract connections | What Works Clearinghouse | Teacher-verified example and alternate explanation | No runtime academic-content generation | Uses an approved example or explanation for a concept |
| Checking understanding without punishment | Active retrieval | What Works Clearinghouse | Optional concept check in Focus Journey | Supportive feedback only; no leaderboard or score | Selects an answer and chooses whether to continue |
